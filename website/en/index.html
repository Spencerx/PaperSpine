03

Product introduction

Paper production should not vanish
inside one generation.

Contributions, motivation, evidence, figures, the canonical manuscript, and publication materials each have their own source of truth and human decision points.

PRODUCT CONTRACT

Install as Skills or plugins. Interact through a local web workspace. Keep authoritative state in project files. Figure assembly is not paper completion, and a canonical manuscript is not external submission authorization.

04 / Product guide

From configuration to publication cycle,
one gated step at a time.

One recoverable state connects five stages. Agents advance work; people own contributions, motivation, complete-figure choices, declarations, and all external actions.

  1. 01

    CONFIGURE

    Configuration

    Define the task, materials, language, citations, figure route, and delivery preferences.

  2. 02

    ANCHOR

    Semantic anchors

    Confirm contribution and motivation before they constrain downstream claims.

  3. 03

    FIGURE

    Scientific figures

    Author complete candidates, review them as complete figures, and preserve decisions.

  4. 04

    CANONICAL

    Canonical manuscript

    Consume figures in the paper, then verify final pixels and five readiness dimensions.

  5. 05

    PUBLICATION

    Publication cycle

    Prepare target, revision, and transfer materials locally. There is no auto-submit button.
